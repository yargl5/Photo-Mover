# Third-party software

The MTP bridge uses [MediaDevices](https://github.com/Bassman2/MediaDevices),
version 1.10.2, by Ralf Beckers, under the MIT license.
The original binary is from the official NuGet package `MediaDevices/1.10.2`
(`lib/net45/MediaDevices.dll`). License text is in `vendor/MediaDevices/LICENSE`.

The packaged application also includes Python, Tcl/Tk and the PyInstaller bootloader,
distributed under their respective licenses.
